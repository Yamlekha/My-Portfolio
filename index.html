My story

About Me

I completed a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering in 2019. Following graduation, I worked as a Maintenance Engineer in the automotive industry from 2021 to 2023, gaining extensive hands-on exposure to two-wheelers, their components, maintenance requirements, and industrial operations.

In 2024, I pursued a 6-month Diploma through CDAC (Centre for Development of Advanced Computing), covering CAD tools such as SolidWorks, PLC programming, Python, and databases including MongoDB and SQL.

Building on this foundation, I transitioned into design engineering in 2025. I currently work as a Design Engineer specialising in automotive BIW fixtures and advanced SPM design, with SolidWorks as my primary design software.

This journey reflects my adaptability, continuous learning mindset, and ability to successfully transition across different engineering domains.
